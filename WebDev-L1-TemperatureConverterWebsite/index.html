LEARN THE BASICS

Temperature conversion guide

Understand how Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin relate to one another.

CELSIUS TO FAHRENHEIT

°C → °F

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

Example: 25°C = 77°F

FAHRENHEIT TO CELSIUS

°F → °C

°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

Example: 77°F = 25°C

CELSIUS TO KELVIN

°C → K

K = °C + 273.15

Example: 25°C = 298.15 K

KELVIN TO CELSIUS

K → °C

°C = K − 273.15

Example: 298.15 K = 25°C

FAHRENHEIT TO KELVIN

°F → K

K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15

Example: 77°F = 298.15 K

KELVIN TO FAHRENHEIT

K → °F

°F = (K − 273.15) × 9/5 + 32

Example: 298.15 K = 77°F

!

What is absolute zero?

Absolute zero is the lowest possible temperature: 0 K, −273.15°C or −459.67°F. Temperatures below this point are not physically possible.
